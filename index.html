Cardily

Advancing Cardiovascular Health Through Education, Research, and Community

Cardily is a fully digital nonprofit dedicated to advancing cardiovascular health through evidence-based content, original research, community outreach, and global education.

Explore Insights Get Involved

Cardiovascular disease claims 17.9 million lives per year, making it the leading cause of death worldwide. Cardily exists to change that through education, research, outreach, and awareness.

Source: World Health Organization

What We Do

Insights Publication

We publish evidence-based articles on cardiovascular health covering diseases and conditions, prevention, lifestyle, and the latest research and innovation.

Cardiovascular Research

Cardily develops and publishes original cardiovascular data research, bringing meaningful analysis to topics that matter in heart health.

Community Outreach

We engage communities directly through awareness campaigns, cardiovascular health education initiatives, and partnerships with organizations that share our mission.

Scientific Publishing Internship

A structured program where contributors research, write, and publish cardiovascular health content under editorial guidance.

Professional Collaborations

Partnerships with physicians, researchers, and medical professionals serving in advisory and contributory roles.

Newsletter

A cardiovascular-focused newsletter published on cardily.org and delivered directly to your inbox, covering the latest insights, research, and Cardily news.

Our Mission

To advance cardiovascular health through evidence-based education, original research, community outreach, and accessible content for everyone.

Cardily is a fully digital nonprofit that publishes cardiovascular health insights, develops original research, engages communities through outreach, and creates opportunities for students and professionals to contribute to the field. We exist because cardiovascular disease remains the world's leading killer, and education and community action are among the most powerful tools we have against it.

Our Vision

Cardily's long-term vision is to become a leading cardiovascular health platform where evidence-based content meets original research and real community impact. We aim to build a global network of contributors, from students to medical professionals to community advocates, united by a commitment to advancing heart health through knowledge, outreach, and innovation.

Ready to Make an Impact?

Whether you are a student, researcher, writer, medical professional, or community advocate, there is a place for you at Cardily. Explore our latest insights, contribute to our research, join our publishing team, or partner with us on outreach initiatives.
