Data and Statistics

Out of Hospital Cardiac Arrest Registry Metrics

A Cardily data-literacy explainer on out of Hospital Cardiac Arrest Registry Metrics, focused on interpretation rather than invented precision.

2026-10-10 11 min read Intro Both

Key takeaways

  • OHCA metrics include incidence, bystander CPR rate, shockable rhythm proportion, and survival to discharge-each with registry definitions.
  • Denominator is often all EMS-treated arrests or presumed cardiac etiology subsets-compare carefully.
  • Utstein template reporting standardizes comparisons across communities.
  • Survival statistics reflect chain of survival, not hospital brand alone.
  • Race and neighborhood correlate with outcomes via response time and chronic stress-not innate difference.

Metric definition

Out-of-hospital cardiac arrest incidence counts events receiving resuscitation attempts per 100,000 person-years. Case definitions exclude obvious non-cardiac traumas per Utstein criteria variants.

Bystander CPR rate numerator is arrests with documented lay CPR; denominator is witnessed collapses or all OHCA depending on report.

Survival to hospital discharge is a system outcome spanning EMS, PCI capability, and post-arrest care.

Where the data usually come from

CARES registry and local EMS databases feed AHA heart disease and stroke statistical update sections.

CDC WONDER does not capture OHCA detail; specialized registries dominate.

NHANES is irrelevant for acute arrest incidence.

How trends can fool you

Increased AED registration improves documentation, appearing as incidence rise.

COVID-19 fear reduced bystander CPR in some regions-temporary metric shifts.

Hospital withdrawal policies affect discharge survival definitions slightly across eras.

Equity and subgroup reading skills

Lower survival in marginalized neighborhoods links to response times, AED access, and comorbidity burden.

Avoid blaming communities; read metrics as infrastructure investment needs.

Compare age-standardized OHCA incidence where possible.

Questions to ask of any chart

Utstein compliant?

All rhythms or shockable only?

Public vs private EMS mix?

Putting it together

OHCA registry metrics teach acute cardiovascular surveillance distinct from chronic disease prevalence.

Students link chain-of-survival numerators to policy levers (CPR training, telecommunicator CPR).

Not emergency response instruction-call local emergency services in real events.
