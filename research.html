Research

Cardily develops original, educationally framed cardiovascular research: secondary data analysis, literature synthesis, and public methods literacy. Findings are published for learning, not as clinical recommendations.

Research Agenda

Three standing priorities guide what we study and how we communicate results.

Priority 1 Youth and young adults

Cardiovascular risk earlier in the life course

Map how blood pressure, lipids, adiposity, nicotine exposure, sleep, and activity appear in adolescent and young-adult populations, with attention to measurement quality and what public datasets can (and cannot) support.

Priority 2 Education science

What cardiovascular teaching actually changes

Evaluate whether Cardily curricula and workshop formats improve knowledge, risk communication skills, and help-seeking literacy, without overclaiming behavior change.

Priority 3 Open methods

Public literacy for heart statistics and trials

Produce citable briefs on absolute versus relative risk, denominators, age adjustment, and how to read cardiology papers, so students and the public can audit headlines themselves.

Methods and Integrity

How Cardily keeps research educationally honest.

Standard Required on all studies

Claims we refuse

We do not invent precise statistics, overstate causality from observational patterns, or present educational findings as personalized medical advice. Absolute risk and denominators are preferred whenever numbers are discussed.

  • Sources Major societies, public-health agencies, peer-reviewed syntheses
  • Limits Every brief states what the data cannot answer
  • Review Student draft + editorial pass; clinical advisors when content warrants
  • Correction Material errors get a dated note on the public page

Collaborate

Students, researchers, and clinicians can contribute to literature reviews, data briefs, and methods teaching. Structured fellowship applications open November 2026.
